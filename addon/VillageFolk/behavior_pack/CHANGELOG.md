[1.1.0] - Mood Update 
- Villagers now have 4 moods: Happy, Neutral, Scared and Sad
- Mood symbol and label shown beside profession on name tag
- Mood detected based on nearby threats, weather and time of day
- Nearby threats within 20 blocks triggers Scared mood
  (Zombie, Skeleton, Creeper, Pillager, Ravager, Witch, Phantom, Drowned)
- Rain or thunder triggers Sad mood
- Nighttime triggers Scared mood
- Daytime triggers Happy mood
- Dawn and dusk default to Neutral mood
- Mood dialogue replaces profession dialogue when mood is not neutral
- 15 unique dialogue lines per mood (45 total mood lines)
- Mood updates automatically every 5 seconds
